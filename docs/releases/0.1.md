The first public release of GATOR, a macro-oscillator for the FM-1 inspired by Mutable Instruments Plaits
(its DSP is Plaits' open-source code by Emilie Gillet; not affiliated with or endorsed by Mutable Instruments).

- 24 synthesis models, low-pass gate, OUT / AUX mix and stereo spread
- ADSR envelope with times in ms / s (1 ms … 10 s) and speed-dependent encoder steps
- two LFOs, an 8-slot modulation matrix, GATE / TRIG / DRONE play modes, HOLD, glide, legato
- 16 presets, USB-MIDI and TRS MIDI in
- 48 kHz audio
