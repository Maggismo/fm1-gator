0.8: a running 16-step call/answer sequencer.

- The 16 white keys are the 16 steps. A tap sets a call on that step and its answer on the next; a tap on either
  takes the pair out. Pairs anywhere, as many as fit.
- Black keys: F#3 both sounds new · G#3 the call's · A#3 the answer's · C#4 new notes · D#4 back;
  G#4 A#4 C#5 D#5 minor, dorian, phrygian, minor pentatonic.
- Call and answer always on two different Plaits models. SELECT / ALGO pick them by hand from all 24.
- Play page: K1 tone, K2 length, K3 octave, K4 variation, PRESET nudge (ms, later / earlier).
  Sound page (ARP): K1-K4 the voices' harmonics and timbre, PRESET the tempo.
- PLAY on the clock: mute (the steps run on, back in at the right place); without a clock: start / stop.
  Start / Continue from the Digitakt: from the top / where it was.
- No delay or reverb of its own any more (use the Digitakt's); the safety filters stay.
