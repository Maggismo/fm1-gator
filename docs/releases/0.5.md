0.5 (Maggismo/fm1-gator): rolled sounds, safety, two pages.

- Starts on the call/answer page (SEQ). ARP: the sound page.
- F3 all new: the figure and both sounds. A3 / C4 tapped: new calls / answers; held half a second: a new call /
  answer sound. E4 back: the figure and the sounds.
- Sounds rolled from two sets of Plaits models, each with its own safe ranges and an evened-out level:
  call 2-OP FM, 6-OP FM I-III, wavetable, modal, string, chiptune, phase distortion, waveshaper;
  answer virtual analog, VA VCF, chords, string machine, harmonic, formant, wave terrain.
- Play page: K1 density, K2 variation, K3 tone, K4 length; SELECT delay, ALGO reverb, PRESET octave; OCT-/OCT+ key.
- Sound page: SELECT / ALGO the call's / answer's model (round), K1 K2 the call's harmonics / timbre, K3 K4 the
  answer's; PRESET the tempo (without a clock).
- SAVE on these pages saves at once. No shifted controls (REC held) any more.
- Safety: no MIDI notes or controllers from outside (only clock and Start/Stop); notes always struck, never held
  (a DRONE preset too); all notes off at start and stop; notes folded into A1..C6; on the output a high-pass at
  35 Hz, a low-pass at 8 kHz and a soft limiter. Delay and reverb are sends: turned down, they ring out.
