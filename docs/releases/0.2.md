**Call/answer generator** (this fork, Maggismo/fm1-gator): SEQ opens its page.

- A short figure two (or four) beats long: a high, short, open **call** and 1-3 sixteenths later a lower, fuller,
  resolved **answer**, now and then twice. Calls and answers sit off the beats (where a drum machine's kick is).
- The call plays one preset, the answer another (SELECT and ALGO on the page; factory P15 CALL, P16 ANSWER).
- Follows MIDI clock and Start/Stop (USB or TRS), phase-locked to it; or runs on its own tempo (C4).
- Keys on the page: F3 all new, G3 new calls, A3 new answers, B3 back (again: forward), C4 run/stop, D4 two/four
  beats; F#3 G#3 A#3 C#4 minor, dorian, phrygian, minor pentatonic; OCT-/OCT+ the key.
- Knobs: K1 density, K2 variation (each round a little different, the figure kept), K3 register (-2..+2 octaves; the call around C3-D4, the answer an octave under it), K4 tempo.
- Saved with the bank (SAVE). A bank from 0.1 keeps its presets.
